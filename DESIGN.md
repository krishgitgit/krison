# KRISON DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand Personality

KRISON should feel:

- Industrial

- Premium

- Precise

- Engineering-focused

- Reliable

- International

- Modern

## Visual Language

The design should draw inspiration from:

- Road markings

- Industrial machinery

- Engineering drawings

- Infrastructure

- Asphalt

- Precision

- Forward movement

## Colors

Primary:

KRISON RED

Supporting:

- Asphalt charcoal

- White

- Off-white

- Industrial gray

- Sky/environmental blue

Do not introduce random gradients or unrelated colors.

## Typography

Use a modern technical sans-serif.

Typography should be strong, clean and highly legible.

## Photography

Prioritize real KRISON machinery photography.

Do not replace available real product photographs with generic AI-generated machinery.

## Hero

Use the supplied bridge/road-marking artwork.

The artwork is a background asset.

Do not bake:

- Navigation

- Logo

- Headline

- Buttons

- Statistics

- Product information

into the background image.

These must remain HTML/CSS.

## Animation

Animation should communicate:

- Precision

- Movement

- Engineering

- Progress

Preferred:

- Image reveals

- Scroll reveals

- Subtle parallax

- Product transitions

- Line-based transitions

- Micro-interactions

Avoid:

- Excessive bouncing

- Excessive 3D

- Random floating elements

- Distracting motion

- Generic SaaS animations

## Responsive Design

Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts must be intentionally designed.

Do not simply scale the desktop layout down.
